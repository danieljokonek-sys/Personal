About

Daniel
Okonek

Sound is a form of knowing. Daniel Okonek has spent his career deepening that knowledge — through the stage, the studio, and the classroom — arriving at a practice rooted in intention, craft, and the quiet authority of someone who has listened very closely for a very long time.

Over 300 live performances have taken him from intimate rooms to festival stages, including sets at Northern Nights and Dirty Bird Campout — experiences that refined his ear for how music inhabits space and moves through bodies.

In the studio, Daniel works across composition, arrangement, production, and mixing, approaching each project as a collaboration toward a specific and irreducible sonic truth. As co-founder of Well Made Plays — an artist management and events company — and co-host of Sound Squeeze Creative, he remains deeply embedded in the broader ecology of independent music and creative community.

His teaching carries the same precision: one-on-one instruction in Ableton Live, built around the belief that technical mastery and artistic vision should develop together.
