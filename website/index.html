About
"Music allows us to communicate emotion with a precision that no other language can emulate."

Daniel
Okonek

Daniel Okonek has spent his career building tools for that communication — through the stage, the studio, and the classroom — arriving at a practice rooted in intention, craft, and the quiet authority of someone who has listened very closely for a very long time.

Over 300 live performances have taken him from intimate rooms to festival stages at Northern Nights / Dirty Bird Campout — experiences that refined his ear for how music inhabits space and moves through bodies.

As co-founder of Well Made Plays — an artist management and events company — and co-host of Sound Squeeze, a creative retreat, Daniel works at the intersection of sound, artistry, and community.

His teaching carries the same precision: one-on-one instruction in Ableton Live, built around the belief that technical mastery and artistic vision should develop together.
